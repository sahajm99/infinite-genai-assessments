ANTICOAGULATION — CLINICAL REFERENCE (SYNTHETIC / FICTIONAL)

Warfarin monitoring.
Warfarin therapy is monitored using the International Normalized Ratio (INR). For most
indications, including atrial fibrillation and treatment of venous thromboembolism, the
target INR range is 2.0 to 3.0. For patients with a mechanical mitral valve, a higher
target INR of 2.5 to 3.5 is used.

Direct oral anticoagulants (DOACs).
DOACs such as apixaban, rivaroxaban, dabigatran, and edoxaban do not require routine INR
monitoring. They are preferred over warfarin for non-valvular atrial fibrillation in many
patients. DOACs are not recommended for patients with mechanical heart valves.

Reversal.
The reversal agent for warfarin is vitamin K, with 4-factor prothrombin complex
concentrate used for major bleeding. Idarucizumab reverses dabigatran. Andexanet alfa
reverses factor Xa inhibitors such as apixaban and rivaroxaban.

Bleeding risk.
The HAS-BLED score estimates bleeding risk in patients with atrial fibrillation who are
being considered for anticoagulation.
