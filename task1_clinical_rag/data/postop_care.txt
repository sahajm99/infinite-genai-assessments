POSTOPERATIVE CARE — CLINICAL REFERENCE (SYNTHETIC / FICTIONAL)

VTE prophylaxis.
After major surgery, venous thromboembolism prophylaxis is recommended. Options include
pharmacologic prophylaxis with low-molecular-weight heparin and mechanical prophylaxis
with intermittent pneumatic compression devices. The choice depends on bleeding risk.

Pain management.
A multimodal analgesia approach is preferred: scheduled acetaminophen and NSAIDs where
not contraindicated, with opioids reserved for breakthrough pain to minimize opioid use.

Early mobilization.
Early ambulation after surgery reduces the risk of pneumonia, venous thromboembolism, and
ileus, and is encouraged as soon as the patient is stable.

Surgical site infection prevention.
Prophylactic antibiotics should be given within 60 minutes before incision and generally
discontinued within 24 hours after surgery. Maintaining normothermia and glycemic control
also reduces surgical site infection risk.
