# CloudNimbus — Product FAQ (SYNTHETIC)

## Billing

**How does billing work?**
CloudNimbus bills monthly on the first of each month. You are charged based on your plan
tier plus any usage overages. Invoices are emailed and available under Settings → Billing.

**What plans are available?**
There are three plans: Starter ($19/month, 1 seat), Team ($99/month, up to 10 seats), and
Business ($499/month, up to 50 seats). Annual billing gives a 20% discount on all plans.

**Can I get a refund?**
Yes. We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on the first payment of any new subscription.
Refund requests after 30 days are prorated only for annual plans that are cancelled early.

## Account

**How do I reset my password?**
Click "Forgot password" on the login page. A reset link is emailed and is valid for 60
minutes. If it expires, request a new one. Password must be at least 12 characters.

**How do I add team members?**
On the Team or Business plan, go to Settings → Members → Invite. Invited members receive an
email invitation valid for 7 days.

## API

**What are the API rate limits?**
The Starter plan allows 60 requests per minute. The Team plan allows 600 requests per
minute. The Business plan allows 3,000 requests per minute. Exceeding the limit returns an
HTTP 429 response.

**How do I export my data?**
Go to Settings → Data → Export. Exports are generated as a downloadable ZIP of JSON files
and typically complete within 15 minutes. Exports are available on Team and Business plans.

## Support

**What are your support hours?**
Email support is available 24/7 with a target first response within 12 hours. Business plan
customers also get priority live chat during business hours (9am–6pm US Eastern).
